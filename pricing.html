Pricing questions

Not sure which plan fits? Talk to us and we'll recommend one honestly.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta, Google or TikTok from your own account, so you keep full ownership and transparency. Our fee covers strategy, creative and management.

How long is the contract?

Retainers run month to month after an initial 3-month period. That gives us enough time to set up properly and show real results.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move up or down a plan at the start of any month. Most clients start on Starter or Growth and scale up once results come in.

How does quarterly billing work?

Pay for three months up front and save 10% on your retainer. Quarterly plans still include a monthly review and report.

Do you offer one-off projects?

Yes. Shoots, websites, brand identity and launch campaigns can all be booked without a retainer.
