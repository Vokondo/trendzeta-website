      Common questions

      How soon will I see results?

      Paid campaigns usually show movement in the first 2–4 weeks. Organic social and SEO build over 3–6 months. We agree on clear targets for each stage before we start.

      Is ad spend included in your packages?

      No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta, Google or TikTok from your own account, so you keep full ownership and transparency. Our fee covers strategy, creative and management.

      Do I have to sign a long contract?

      Retainers run month to month after an initial 3-month period. That gives us enough time to set up properly and show real results.

      Do you only work with businesses in Ghana?

      Most of our clients are in Ghana, but we also run campaigns across West Africa and for diaspora-focused brands in the UK and US.

      What do you need from me to get started?

      Access to your social and ad accounts, any brand assets you have, and a 45-minute kickoff call. We handle the rest.
